## 核心方法

2 Methodology Figure 2: Overall architecture. (a) The base generator takes text tokens, a reference image latent, and noisy video and audio tokens as input, and jointly denoises the video and audio tokens with a single-stream Transformer. All modalities are processed within a unified token sequence using self-attention only, without separate cross-attention or fusion modules. A latent-space super-resolution stage can be further applied to refine the generated video at higher output resolutions. (b) The single-stream Transformer adopts a sandwich architecture layout: the first and last 4 layers use modality-specific projections and normalization parameters, while the middle 32 layers share the main Transformer parameters across modalities.
